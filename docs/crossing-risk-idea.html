A self-training loop for self-driving cars

Not just detection: a pipeline that finds risky moments in fleet video, scores them, packages each one for a simulator to multiply, and trains the next model. Today a person labels each case; next, auto triage calls people in only for dangerous or uncertain ones.

Click a box or a step. Use ← → to walk through.

  1. Car video → our pipeline → databaseDashcam clips sit in S3 on VAST. Our pipeline reads them and writes every event into the database.
  2. Detect the scenarioToday: a pedestrian crossing. Stored YOLO boxes, then Cosmos3-Reason on the clip, then Nemotron VL on one frame. Model calls are traced in W&B.
  3. Score itDanger 0–100, plus what the driver did vs what the model suggests. Scores go into the database.
  4. Detectors for any scenario, in plain wordsPedestrian crossing is the built-in detector. Anyone can write a new one in plain words, test it on one clip (Cosmos, about 5 s) and run it over the clips. Next: tuned detectors with their own danger score for trucks, jams, road works and collisions.
  5. People label cases; auto triage is nextToday a person can label any case: driver right, model right, both fine or false alarm. Next: confident low-risk cases are labelled automatically, and only dangerous or unsure ones (high danger, driver and model disagree, low confidence) go to a person.
  6. Each case is packaged for a simulator (send is a demo)Every case worth learning from becomes a scenario package (real today, with Download JSON). Sending it to a simulator is a demo: nothing is connected.
  7. 1,000+ variants per case (next)Built today: each package lists suggested variations (night, rain, a child, a faster car, a later step-out, a hidden pedestrian) and a generation prompt for Cosmos Transfer. Generating the variants is next.
  8. Train model n (next)Every round makes a new version: v1, v2, v3 and so on.
  9. The model goes back into the car (next)The new version drives in shadow mode next to the human driver.
  10. New videos are ingested: the loop is closed (next)The car records new drives, they are ingested into the platform, and step 1 starts again with a better model.
